# Boldr — Product Narrative (one page)

## The problem
Support tickets get answered and forgotten. Every day, customers tell you exactly
what confuses them, what they're worried about, and what they wish your product
pages said — and all of that intelligence evaporates the moment a reply is sent.

## What Boldr is
**A Customer Intelligence Engine.** Not a chatbot. Every support ticket becomes
four things at once:

- a **response** to the customer,
- a **knowledge asset** the company keeps,
- a **customer signal** about who's buying and why,
- a **marketing opportunity** grounded in real demand.

## Why it's valuable
Support stops being a cost center and becomes a **growth channel**. The same
inbox that answers questions now tells you which product pages to fix, which
segments are emerging, and which campaigns will land — backed by evidence, not
guesses. And it **compounds**: every answered question makes the next one cheaper
to answer.

## How it works — one loop, five stages

```
        Customer Question
               │
        ┌──────▼───────┐
        │   ANSWER      │  understand intent + persona, retrieve, score
        │               │  confidence, route, draft a cited reply
        └──────┬───────┘
        ┌──────▼───────┐
        │    LEARN      │  novel questions become reviewed FAQ entries
        │               │  that publish back into the knowledge base
        └──────┬───────┘
        ┌──────▼───────┐
        │   DISCOVER    │  cluster tickets into themes + buyer segments
        └──────┬───────┘
        ┌──────▼───────┐
        │     ACT       │  themes → marketing briefs → persona campaigns
        └──────┬───────┘
        ┌──────▼───────┐
        │   MEASURE     │  internal demand vs external market sentiment
        └──────────────┘
```

Every screen in the product is one stage of this loop:

| Stage | What it is | Screen |
|---|---|---|
| **Answer** | Customer intelligence collection | Inbox / Live pipeline |
| **Learn** | Customer intelligence learning | Knowledge gaps |
| **Discover** | Customer intelligence analysis | Themes / Personas |
| **Act** | Customer intelligence activation | Marketing brief / Campaigns |
| **Measure** | Customer intelligence validation | External benchmark |

There is **one underlying object** — the intelligence record for a single ticket
(`/intelligence/[ticketId]`). Every screen above is just a *view* of those
records, rolled up a different way. That's why everything traces back to a real
customer question.

## The business outcome
- **Less support effort** — most replies are drafted, not written from scratch.
- **Better product pages** — the gaps customers hit become page fixes.
- **Better campaigns** — built from real, evidenced demand.
- **A knowledge base that grows itself** — every gap closed once is reused forever.

## The one-line pitch
> **Boldr turns every customer question into product and marketing intelligence —
> and gets cheaper at it every day.**
