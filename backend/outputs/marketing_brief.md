# Boldr Supply Co. — Monthly Customer Intelligence Brief

**Period:** Monthly Snapshot | **Total Tickets:** 70 | **Reading time:** ~8 min

---

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Strap questions are your biggest conversion blocker.** 23 of 70 tickets (33%) concern strap safety, compatibility, and sizing — driven by health-conscious and active buyers who are pre-purchase and need reassurance that doesn't exist on your product pages.
- **Gifters are ready to spend more; you're leaving it on the table.** 13 tickets across engraving and gift presentation came exclusively from the gifter persona. No engraving configurator, no gift wrapping preview, no dedicated page — every one of those tickets is a friction point before a high-intent purchase.
- **Automation is critically underdeployed.** Only 6 of 70 tickets (9%) were auto-resolved. Nine knowledge gaps remain unaddressed. The top three gap clusters alone account for 49 tickets that are largely answerable with static content.

---

## 2. What Customers Are Asking That Isn't on Your Product Pages

### Watch Strap Safety and Compatibility
**33% of all tickets. Your largest single content gap.**

Customers need material safety and sizing information to complete a purchase. It isn't there, so they ask — or they leave.

**Persona mix:** Health-conscious (9), Active (6), Enthusiast (6), Sustainable (2)

**Verbatim samples:**
> "Strap dye safety"
> "Strap length for large wrist"

**Product-page recommendation:** Add a persistent "Strap Guide" tab on every product page — not a separate URL, on the page itself. Include: (1) skin-safe/hypoallergenic certification status per strap material, (2) swim/sweat suitability rating, (3) sizing by wrist circumference in mm, (4) a compatibility matrix showing which straps fit which case lugs. If you have certifications, badge them visibly above the fold. If you don't, get them — this is your dominant segment asking.

---

### Service Costs and Logistics
**15 tickets. Operational friction eroding post-purchase trust.**

Buyers — particularly active users who will service repeatedly — cannot find basic aftercare pricing or turnaround times anywhere on the site.

**Persona mix:** Active (8), Enthusiast (5), Gifter (2)

**Verbatim samples:**
> "Water resistance re-testing after service"
> "Servicing turnaround time"

**Product-page recommendation:** Add a "Watch Care" accordion section to every product page, above the footer. Minimum content: battery replacement cost, regulation service cost, water resistance re-test availability and cost post-service, standard and express turnaround times, return shipping options. This is also a trust signal for first-time buyers evaluating long-term ownership cost.

---

### Watch Engraving Service Questions
**11 tickets. 100% gifter persona. 100% pre-purchase friction.**

Every engraving question came from a gifter. They are ready to buy a personalised product that you already offer — they just cannot find the details.

**Persona mix:** Gifter (11)

**Verbatim samples:**
> "Engraving in Chinese characters"
> "Multi-line engraving"

**Product-page recommendation:** Build an engraving configurator directly on the product page — not a separate FAQ. Fields: placement (caseback / strap), script selection (Latin / Chinese characters explicitly listed), character limit per line, line limit, per-character or flat-fee pricing, turnaround time. Add a live preview if budget allows; if not, show static placement examples. Promote this in the product image carousel with a dedicated "Personalise It" slide.

---

### Order Issues and Fulfillment
**8 tickets. Operational. All from active buyers.**

Post-purchase problems — lost parcels, wrong items, discount codes failing — are hitting one persona hard and generating avoidable support load.

**Persona mix:** Active (8)

**Verbatim samples:**
> "Wrong item received"
> "Order not received - presumed lost"

**Product-page recommendation:** This is not a product-page fix — it is a self-service portal requirement. Add a prominent "Manage My Order" link in order confirmation emails and the site header. At minimum, surface: live tracking, address amendment window, wrong-item report form, and refund initiation. Until the portal exists, add a dedicated Order Issues FAQ page linked from the confirmation email so customers are not searching blind.

---

### Titanium Product Value and Practices
**4 tickets. Small volume, high-value segment.**

Enthusiasts are asking about grade specifications, resale value, and sustainability sourcing — the exact questions that justify premium pricing. None of this is on the site.

**Persona mix:** Enthusiast (3), Sustainable (1)

**Verbatim samples:**
> "Titanium grade on the Expedition model"
> "Resale value of titanium watches"

**Product-page recommendation:** Add a "Material Spec" section to each product page listing the exact titanium grade (e.g. Grade 5 / 6Al-4V), sourcing origin, and finishing process. Link to a brand-level "Why Titanium" content page covering durability data, resale value context, and sustainability sourcing. This content does double duty: it answers enthusiast questions and justifies price to every visitor.

---

### Shipping Costs and Environmental Impact
**2 tickets. Sustainable persona. Low volume, high signal.**

Two customers asked who pays customs duties and what the carbon footprint of shipping is. These are not edge cases — they are early indicators of a segment that will grow.

**Persona mix:** Active (1), Sustainable (1)

**Verbatim samples:**
> "Customs duties - who pays?"
> "Carbon footprint of shipping"

**Product-page recommendation:** Add a "Shipping & Duties" expandable section to the cart and checkout pages. State clearly: which regions incur import duties, who is liable, and estimated duty ranges for key markets (SG, US, UK, EU, AU). Add one line on carbon offset options if you offer them. If you don't offer them, decide whether you will — this segment will ask again.

---

### Gift Presentation and Personalisation
**2 tickets. Gifter persona. Upsell opportunity with zero current infrastructure.**

Customers want gift wrapping and personalisation options at checkout. Neither is visible.

**Persona mix:** Gifter (2)

**Verbatim samples:**
> "Gift wrapping"
> "Gifting - personalisation options"

**Product-page recommendation:** Add a "Gift Options" add-on module to the product page and cart — gift wrapping (with a photo of the actual box), gift message field, and a link to the engraving configurator. Price it clearly. This is a direct revenue line, not just a service improvement.

---

### Extreme Sports Watch Performance
**2 tickets. Active persona. Messaging gap, not a product gap.**

Adventure buyers are evaluating whether Boldr watches meet their specific performance requirements. The specs exist; the targeted messaging does not.

**Persona mix:** Active (2)

**Verbatim samples:**
> "Watch for extreme sports"
> "Altitude performance"

**Product-page recommendation:** On relevant models (Expedition and equivalents), add a "Performance Specs" section listing: water resistance depth, shock resistance rating, altitude range tested, lume type and duration, and case material impact resistance. Use the language active buyers use — "dive-ready," "trail-tested," "altitude-rated" — not generic marketing copy.

---

### Exclusive Watchmaker Partnerships and Limited Editions
**2 tickets. Enthusiast persona. Demand signal for a product line you may not be maximising.**

Enthusiasts are actively asking about collaborations and limited drops. There is no page to send them to and no waitlist to capture them.

**Persona mix:** Enthusiast (2)

**Verbatim samples:**
> "Collaboration with independent watchmakers"
> "Limited edition availability"

**Product-page recommendation:** Create a "Collaborations" landing page — even if it is a single coming-soon page with a waitlist email capture. Add a navigation link to it now. Every enthusiast who asks this question and finds nothing is a missed retention opportunity.

## 3. Persona Shift Signals

| Signal | Implication |
|---|---|
| **Active** is now your largest persona at 25 tickets (36%), up from what the gifter and enthusiast segments would suggest as the natural lead. | Shift campaign spend toward active lifestyle use cases. Strap compatibility and service logistics are their primary blockers. |
| **Gifter** at 15 tickets (21%) is concentrated entirely in engraving and presentation questions — no product questions. | Gifters know what they want to buy. Remove the information friction and conversion follows. Prioritise before Father's Day and Christmas. |
| **Health-conscious** at 9 tickets (13%) is punching above its volume weight — all 9 tickets went to the same gap cluster. | This persona is underserved and self-selecting. A single strap safety page could capture all of them. |
| **Sustainable** at 4 tickets (6%) is small but asking specific, informed questions (carbon footprint, sourcing practices). | Early adopter signal. Content investment
