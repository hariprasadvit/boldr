# BOLDR SUPPLY CO. - Customer Service SOP
Internal reference for CS team | Not for external distribution
Last updated: January 2026

## 1. Overview
This document describes how our customer service team handles inbound enquiries today. We receive enquiries primarily by email through our Gmail inbox. All replies are sent manually by a CS team member after checking the relevant reference documents.

## 2. Reference Documents
- FAQ Document - Common questions on returns, shipping, warranty, materials, care
- Engraving Rate Card - Pricing, character limits, turnaround, script types
- Servicing Rate Card - Battery, regulation, full service pricing and turnaround
- Product One-Pager - Model specs, materials, strap options, compatibility
- Shopify Admin - Order status, tracking, fulfilment, refund status

## 3. Tone & Brand Voice
- Friendly but not overly casual - we are a premium brand
- Direct - answer the question clearly, do not pad with filler
- Helpful - if we cannot help directly, point them somewhere useful
- Never promise what you are not sure about - check first

Good example reply opening: "Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out! Happy to help with that."

Avoid:
- Starting every reply with "Great question!"
- Using overly formal language ("Dear Sir/Madam")
- Making promises about timelines you cannot guarantee

## 4. Escalation Triggers
Escalate to the team lead whenever:
- A customer is angry or threatening a chargeback
- A warranty claim involves significant damage or potential product defect
- A refund has been outstanding for more than 10 business days
- A corporate or bulk order enquiry comes in (more than 5 units)
- You are not sure of the answer and cannot find it in the reference documents
- A media or press enquiry comes in - forward to marketing@boldr.co

## 5. Order Handling Rules
- Always check Shopify first before replying. Do not guess order status.
- Tracking not updating after 5+ business days: escalate to shipping carrier
- Wrong item received: ask for a photo, flag to team immediately
- Change delivery address: only possible if not yet dispatched
- Cancel order: if unfulfilled, cancel and refund. If shipped, ask customer to return it
- Customs duties: customer is responsible

## 6. Engraving Rules
- Within 1 hour of order: amend for free
- 1-24 hours after order: SGD 15 amendment fee
- After production has started: not possible
- Logo/custom artwork: requires vector file, team approval
- Engraved items are non-returnable unless manufacturing defect

## 7. New Questions Log
If a customer asks something not in any reference document:
1. Reply honestly: "Let me check on that and get back to you."
2. Add to New Questions Log
3. Discuss at weekly CS check-in
4. Once answered, update the FAQ document
