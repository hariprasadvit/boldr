# External Sentiment Benchmark

Twelve internal ticket themes (n = 81 total tickets) were benchmarked against external signals drawn from r/Watches, WatchUSeek, and Trustpilot. The goal: determine which pain points are Boldr-specific execution gaps versus market-wide frustrations Boldr is already positioned to win. Each theme is classified and assigned a single next action.

### Engraving Options

Internal: 15 tickets requesting engraving, mostly gifting context. External: market-wide frustration that slow turnaround (some brands: 4 weeks) kills the gift use case — Boldr's 2–3 day standard and same-day rush is already ahead.

> *"Engraving turnaround time is the make-or-break for gift purchases. Some brands take 4 weeks which kills the gift use case."*
> — r/Watches

**Action:** Launch a dedicated gifting campaign for Q4 that leads with "Engraved in 2–3 days / same-day rush available" as the primary headline claim, not a buried FAQ entry.

---

### Nickel Allergy Straps

Internal: 6 tickets from customers specifically asking whether buckles are nickel-free, not just straps. External: high-signal negative sentiment confirming that even brands claiming "hypoallergenic" fail at the buckle — an unsolved market gap Boldr's titanium buckle already closes.

> *"Why is it so hard to find a watch where every strap option is nickel-free? Even brands that say hypoallergenic still use buckles with nickel."*
> — WatchUSeek

**Action:** Add an explicit "100% nickel-free: FKM strap + titanium buckle" badge to every affected product page and surface it in the strap filter/search layer.

---

### Water Resistance Real-World

Internal: 9 tickets asking what water resistance actually means in practice. External: high-signal negative sentiment across the market — consumers are burned by misleading ISO 22810 interpretations and distrust brand claims universally.

> *"100m water resistance ratings are misleading. ISO 22810 means it survives static pressure not actual swimming. Brands need to be clearer."*
> — WatchUSeek

**Action:** Publish a single authoritative "What our water resistance rating actually means" explainer page and link it inline from every product's spec table, not just a help-centre article nobody finds.

---

**BOTTOM LINE:** Boldr's three fastest wins are already built into the product — engraving speed, nickel-free hardware, and honest water-resistance language — none are visible enough, and fixing that costs zero in R&D.
