# Boldr Supply Co. — Monthly Customer Intelligence Brief

---

## 1. Executive Summary

- **70 tickets** processed this period. Auto-reply handled 26% (18); 60% (42) required human review; 14% (10) exposed knowledge gaps the agent could not resolve.
- **Gifters and health-conscious buyers are your highest-intent segments this month** — they are asking specific, purchase-blocking questions (engraving pricing, BPA status, allergen certifications) that your product pages do not answer. These are lost conversions, not general curiosity.
- **Single biggest opportunity:** Consolidate the three engraving-related gap clusters (options, pricing, language/fonts) into one conversion-ready engraving guide — gifters represent 15 of 70 tickets and are signaling purchase intent on every contact.

## 2. What Customers Are Asking That Isn't on Your Product Pages

*Sorted by combined volume + commercial value.*

---

### Watch Engraving Options, Pricing, and Language

**Combined volume: 7 tickets | Personas: gifter (dominant)**

Gifters are blocked at the point of purchase by three unanswered questions: where can the engraving go, what does it cost, and can it be done in non-Latin script. These are the same persona, the same intent, and the same missing page — split across two gap clusters artificially.

| Persona | Tickets |
|---|---|
| Gifter | 7 |

**Verbatim customer questions:**
- *"Engraving price per character"*
- *"Engraving in Chinese characters"*
- *"Multi-line engraving"*
- *"Font options for engraving"*

**Recommendation:** Build a single `/engraving` landing page before the next gifting peak. It must include: placement options (caseback, strap), character limits, multi-line availability, font gallery, per-character pricing calculator, and a confirmed list of supported scripts (Chinese, Arabic, Latin minimum). Link to it from every product page above the fold under a "Personalise This Watch" CTA. This is one page, not a FAQ addition — gifters share links, and a shareable configurator page drives referral.

### Watch Strap Material Safety and Ethics

**Combined volume: 7 tickets | Personas: health_conscious (6), sustainable (1)**

Health-conscious buyers are asking questions your competitors probably answer in a single badge line. They are not browsing — they need one specific reassurance before converting.

| Persona | Tickets |
|---|---|
| Health-conscious | 6 |
| Sustainable | 1 |

**Verbatim customer questions:**
- *"Is the watch strap BPA-free?"*
- *"Strap for sensitive skin"*
- *"Vegan-friendly materials"*
- *"Hypoallergenic claim"*

**Recommendation:** Add four product-page badges to all strap listings this month: BPA-Free, Hypoallergenic, Nickel-Free, Vegan-Certified (or clearly flag which certifications you hold vs. do not hold — false claims are worse than silence). Add a collapsible "Strap Materials & Safety" section to each product page with dye process detail, silicone grade, and skin-sensitivity suitability. Do not bury this in a general FAQ — health-conscious buyers scan product pages, not help centers.

### Expedition Model Materials and Comparison

**Volume: 4 tickets | Personas: enthusiast (2), active (2)**

Buyers are in active research mode comparing Expedition vs. Journey and titanium vs. stainless steel. This is pre-purchase, high-intent behavior. They are not finding the answer on your site, so they are asking support — or leaving.

| Persona | Tickets |
|---|---|
| Enthusiast | 2 |
| Active | 2 |

**Verbatim customer questions:**
- *"Titanium grade on the Expedition model"*
- *"Difference between Expedition and Journey"*
- *"Watch for extreme sports"*

**Recommendation:** Add a comparison block directly to the Expedition product page — not a separate page requiring navigation. A tight spec table (titanium grade, weight, case thickness, water resistance, shock rating) with a one-paragraph "Who this is for" section is sufficient. Cross-link Journey from Expedition and vice versa. If you have drop or scratch test data, surface it here — active buyers respond to proof, not adjectives.

### Watch Strap Compatibility and Sizing

**Volume: 3 tickets | Personas: enthusiast (3)**

Enthusiasts customizing straps are your highest-LTV repeat buyers. They are asking lug width and NATO compatibility questions that should be in a spec table, not a support queue.

**Verbatim customer questions:**
- *"Strap width for Expedition 40mm"*
- *"NATO strap compatibility"*
- *"Interchangeable strap between models"*

**Recommendation:** Add a "Strap Compatibility" row to every product page spec table. Include lug width (in mm), confirmed NATO compatibility (yes/no), and a note on cross-model interchangeability. If you sell branded strap accessories, link them here — this is an accessory revenue touchpoint, not just an information gap.

### Order Tracking and Delivery Issues

**Volume: 3 tickets | Personas: active (3)**

Operationally driven. Not a marketing opportunity — a trust erosion risk.

**Verbatim customer questions:**
- *"Tracking number not updating"*
- *"Order not received - presumed lost"*

**Recommendation:** Implement a self-serve "Where Is My Order" (WISMO) portal and trigger automated post-dispatch SMS/email with live tracking links. This reduces inbound volume and stops support agents spending time on carrier delays they cannot control. Address in section 4 below.

---

### Pre and Post Order Management

**Volume: 3 tickets | Personas: active (3)**

Customers cannot self-serve on address changes, cancellations, or refund status. These are avoidable contacts.

**Verbatim customer questions:**
- *"Change delivery address"*
- *"Cancel order before shipping"*
- *"Refund status"*

**Recommendation:** Build a minimal self-service order management portal (address update, cancellation within X hours, refund tracker). Until then, add a prominent post-purchase email that tells customers exactly what to do and what the window is for each action — this sets expectations and reduces panic contacts.

---

### Purchase Protection and Policies

**Volume: 2 tickets | Personas: active (2)**

Warranty and returns questions appearing at the support stage means buyers are not seeing this information at the decision stage.

**Recommendation:** Add a "Buyer Protection" summary — warranty period, return window, key conditions — as a persistent element on all product pages, ideally near the Add to Cart button. One-sentence version with a link to full terms. Pre-purchase visibility of strong policies converts hesitant buyers.

---

### Pricing and Bulk Purchasing

**Volume: 2 tickets | Personas: active (1), gifter (1)**

Low volume this period but commercially high-value signals. Bulk and corporate gifting inquiries warrant a structured response.

**Recommendation:** Create a `/corporate-gifting` landing page with pricing tiers, minimum order quantities, engraving availability at scale, and a contact form. Link from the main navigation under "Gifting." Do not let these inquiries fall into a generic support queue.

---

### Child Safety and Certification

**Volume: 2 tickets | Personas: health_conscious (2)**

Niche but high-stakes. A parent asking about child safety and not getting a clear answer will not buy.

**Recommendation:** If certifications exist (EU safety standards, non-toxic materials), add a "Safety & Certifications" badge to the relevant product page. If certifications do not exist or the product is not positioned for children, add a clear "Designed for adults 18+" note to pre-empt the question and avoid liability exposure.
